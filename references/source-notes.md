# Derivation and limits

This skill is an original synthesis of conditional decision rules, not a concatenation of source prompts. Public sources were reviewed on 2026-11-04; general discussion guidance was refined on 2026-11-06. Historical policy examples are in `venue-mechanisms.md`; they must be refreshed for actual submissions. No confidential manuscript, review transcript, or case-specific result is included in this package.

- **dejian-lc/Rebuttal-Skill**, commit `0088d5e56a035befc65bd8702a24da936e00b8fc`, https://github.com/dejian-lc/Rebuttal-Skill . Retain the two-stage diagnosis/result-integration idea, concern interpretation, experiment ranking, and revision fallback. Replace score-based triage shortcuts with evidence and feasibility judgments. Reduce mandatory output bulk.
- **丁霄汉**, “顶会rebuttal技术浅谈：站着，还把论文中了,” https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/602024489 . The design draws on this article's principles of factual disagreement, decision-maker awareness, and concrete revisions, also examined through an adaptation. The online original was not freshly reverified in the November source audit. Exclude motive inference, adversarial reviewer labels, and conflict-seeking rhetoric. Cross-review agreement is context only.
- **Paper2Rebuttal**, https://arxiv.org/html/2601.14171v2 and https://github.com/AutoLab-SAI-SJTU/Paper2Rebuttal , code commit `f521c7f4383643ca237b3041d6b42a72e947322a`. Retain concern-conditioned evidence retrieval and inspectable plans. The paper reports automated rubric results and a small author study, not a demonstrated causal improvement in conference acceptance. Code `prompts/rebuttal_writer.yaml` and `rebuttal_reviewer.yaml` contain conflicting rules: prohibition of fabricated numbers with `[TBD]`, followed by language about speculative data marked with an asterisk. Their example also assumes outperformance before a result exists. Exclude all such outcome precommitment. The extractor has an exception for specific flaws in ignored fields; still preserve informative metadata elsewhere rather than deleting whole fields.
- **awesome-rebuttal**, https://github.com/xiongqi123123/awesome-rebuttal , commit `3434455fb2460b85793b4e3082bd28bf86ff7323`. Retain source tracking, format adaptation, stable concern/experiment IDs, and discussion deltas. Make state and rehearsal proportional to the task. Replace selection by negative-result risk or `reportable_if_successful` with validity, feasibility, relevance, and reportability independent of favorability. Unknown rules should not block unrelated work.
- **learning_research**, https://github.com/dejian-lc/learning_research , commit `6fdbcdfe24167feb7164d5625a477c75bd118040`. README links to a Notion rebuttal article. Its body was not recovered; no substantive rules are attributed to it here. Include it only after a verifiable export or readable source becomes available.
- **Historical-outcome heuristics:** observed communication needs may guide response structure, but personality labels and historical score-change rates are not adopted as individual predictions or evidence of causal strategy effects. Such use would require traceable data, applicable outcome definitions, and validation.

The public repository includes source attribution and synthetic regression scenarios, not private development records. No acceptance probability or empirically validated superiority is claimed for this synthesis. Behavioral checks are small synthetic smoke tests of important failure cases.
